## Case Study 1: Horten Verft — Shore Power Transition

**Client**: Horten Shipyard & Maritime Services AS  
**Project Duration**: 4 weeks production, 90-second deliverable  
**Focus**: Electrification infrastructure, before/after documentation  
**Enova Compliance**: ✅ Yes

### The Challenge
Horten Verft invested 45 million NOK in shore power infrastructure to eliminate diesel generator use during ship servicing. Without visual proof, investors and regulatory bodies couldn't understand the scale of the transition.

- **Cinematic Aerial Footage**: Drone photography of new shore power installations (cables, transformers, berthing points)
- **Before/After Sequence**: Historical imagery vs. new infrastructure
- **90-Second Transition Film**: Narrated video suitable for board presentations and investor meetings
- **10 High-Resolution Images**: LinkedIn, website, and ESG report ready
- **Enova Documentation Package**: Pre-formatted for subsidy compliance reports

### Impact
- Faster subsidy approval (regulatory clarity)
- 340% increased LinkedIn engagement on sustainability announcement

## Case Study 3: GreenShip Lines — Vessel Battery Electrification

**Client**: GreenShip Lines AS (Regional Shipping Operator)  
**Project Duration**: 5 weeks production, complex multi-site documentation  
**Focus**: Battery system installation, vessel conversion, operational proof  
**Enova Compliance**: ✅ Yes

### The Challenge
GreenShip Lines retrofitted 12 regional ferries with battery systems. Each vessel required new electrical architecture, integration with existing systems, and operational validation. Communicating this to investors, crew, and regulatory bodies required:
- Proof of installation quality
- Operational capability demonstration
- Safety certification validation
- Recruitment/employee communications

### What We Delivered
- **Installation Documentation**: Cinematography of battery modules, electrical integration, and system testing
- **Operational Proof**: Ship launch sequences, performance testing, actual vessel operation footage
- **Employee Communications Film**: 3-minute crew training/orientation content
- **Investor Deck**: 5-minute comprehensive transition story (from retrofit to operation)
- **15 High-Resolution Images**: Crew training, operational procedures, vessel performance

### Impact
- Enabled hiring of 45 new technical staff (clarity on technology job security)
- Shortened insurance approval by 6 weeks (safety footage eliminated underwriter site visits)
- Successfully attracted 120 million NOK private investment for additional conversions
- 95% employee confidence in new systems (trained via visual documentation)

## How These Projects Were Built

Each project follows our proven methodology:

1. **Discovery Phase** (Week 1)
   - Infrastructure assessment and technical requirements
   - Regulatory/Enova documentation review
   - Stakeholder interview (investors, operators, crew)

2. **Production Phase** (Weeks 2-4)
   - Drone cinematography (aerial context)
   - Ground-level industrial filming (technical detail)
   - Integration with existing photos/footage
   - AI-assisted color grading & audio design

3. **Post-Production Phase** (Week 4-5)
   - Multi-format editing (90-sec, 5-min, 30-sec versions)
   - Enova compliance formatting
   - Social media optimization
   - Final delivery (4K masters + compressed formats)
