# GreenLand Production Process

## From Inquiry to Delivery: Your Journey

We understand that timing matters. Here's exactly how we work with you from first contact to final delivery.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery & Scoping (Week 1)

### Your Steps
1. **Initial Inquiry** (Day 1)
   - Email us or complete our inquiry form
   - Brief description: infrastructure type, locations, primary audience
   - Expected budget & timeline

2. **Discovery Call** (Days 2-3)
   - 30-minute video call with our production lead
   - We discuss: infrastructure, stakeholder needs, Enova/regulatory requirements
   - You ask questions about our process

3. **Site Assessment** (Days 3-5)
   - Our team visits your location(s)
   - We document conditions, lighting, access points, safety requirements
   - Technical requirements confirmed (drone permits, special equipment, etc.)

### Our Steps
- **Package Recommendation**: Based on scope, we suggest Standard/Premium/Enterprise
- **Custom Quote**: Detailed breakdown of what we'll deliver and when
- **Timeline Confirmation**: Exact dates for production and delivery
- **Contract Preparation**: Terms, revisions included, licensing rights, payment schedule

### Deliverable from Phase 1
✅ **Signed contract, confirmed production date, detailed scope document**

---

## Phase 2: Production Booking & Planning (Week 2)

### Your Steps
1. **Sign Contract & Deposit Payment** (50% upfront)
2. **Provide Access Information**
   - Key contacts for site access
   - Safety briefing requirements
   - Any operational constraints (dock schedules, weather windows, etc.)

3. **Share Reference Materials**
   - Existing photos of infrastructure
   - Company branding guidelines (logos, colors, fonts)
   - Key stakeholders we should film/interview

### Our Steps
- **Production Plan Development**
  - Shot list creation (drone angles, ground-level sequences, detail shots)
  - Equipment staging and shipping (if off-site)
  - Crew assignment and scheduling
  - Backup plan for weather/contingencies

- **Regulatory Compliance**
  - Drone permits (if required in your region)
  - Maritime authority clearances (if filming at harbor)
  - Safety briefing preparation
  - Insurance verification

- **Pre-Production Materials**
  - Storyboard visual outline
  - Camera angle mockups
  - Timeline and crew schedule
  - Site logistics plan (parking, power access, safety zones)

### Deliverable from Phase 2
✅ **Detailed production plan, confirmed crew, equipment list, site logistics confirmed**

## Phase 3: Production Day(s) (Week 3-4)

### Day-of Timeline (Standard Package = 1 Day)

**6:00 AM - Arrival & Setup**
- Equipment check and staging
- Safety briefing with your team
- Location walkthrough and confirmation
- Weather assessment

**7:00 AM - Drone Pre-Flight**
- Drone flight permit verification
- Altitude/airspace checks
- Initial aerial cinematography begins
- (Typically 2-3 hours of flight time)

**9:00 AM - Ground-Level Filming**
- Wide establishing shots
- Close-up infrastructure documentation
- Detail sequences (cables, connectors, control systems)
- Multi-angle coverage of key areas

**12:00 PM - Lunch Break & Transition**
- Equipment maintenance
- Drone batteries recharged
- Afternoon shot list review

**1:00 PM - Continued Production**
- Additional aerial sequences (if weather permits)
- Crew interviews/testimonials (if applicable)
- Operational footage (vessel, equipment in use)
- Safety system documentation

**4:00 PM - Wrap-Up**
- Final equipment checks
- Site restoration (if needed)
- Backup data verification (confirm all footage captured)
- Site departure

### What We Capture
- **Drone Footage**: 4K, color-graded, multiple angles
- **Ground Cinematography**: Professional multi-camera coverage
- **Detail Sequences**: Close-ups, macro shots, technical elements
- **Operational Proof**: Live systems in use, performance validation
- **Interview Content** (if applicable): Key stakeholders, crew, operators

### Premium/Enterprise Packages
- Extend to 2-5 production days
- Multiple locations documented
- Extended scope with same timeline efficiency

### During Production
**You Receive Daily Updates:**
- Drone footage preview (same-day review)
- Status update email (production on schedule)
- Any issues flagged immediately for problem-solving

### Deliverable from Phase 3
✅ **All raw footage, backed up, organized by location/sequence**

## Phase 4: Post-Production & Editing (Weeks 4-6)

### Timeline Breakdown

**Week 4: Footage Review & Rough Assembly**
- All raw footage reviewed and organized
- Color grading begins (matching footage to create cohesion)
- Rough edit assembled (sequences in order)
- Audio design initiated (sound effects, ambient sound)

**Week 5: Fine Editing & Revisions**
- Transitions, pacing, timing refined
- Visual effects/animations added (if applicable)
- Sound mixing and music selection
- **First Draft Complete**: Sent to you for review

**Week 5-6: Your Review & Revision Round 1**
- You watch the draft (typically in private Vimeo link)
- You provide feedback: pacing, messaging, any changes needed
- We incorporate changes (included up to 2 revision rounds in standard package)

**Week 6: Final Delivery Preparation**
- Multiple export formats prepared:
  - 4K master (archival quality)
  - 1080p versions (web, email, presentations)
  - Social media cuts (30-sec, 15-sec, mobile-optimized)
  - Enova compliance format (if applicable)

### What You See During Post-Production

**Review #1 (Week 5)**
- Complete rough edit of primary film
- Feedback request: major changes, messaging alignment, tone
- Revision turnaround: 3-5 business days

**Review #2 (Week 5-6)**
- Refined edit incorporating your feedback
- Final cosmetic tweaks: color, sound, pacing
- Sign-off review before export

### Deliverable from Phase 4
✅ **Complete edited film, multiple formats, ready for deployment**

## What Can Delay Timeline?

**Rare scenarios that might extend delivery:**

❌ **Weather Holds** (drone filming requires specific conditions)
- Mitigation: We schedule backup days, have contingency plans

❌ **Permitting Delays** (maritime authority clearances)
- Mitigation: Handled during planning phase, rarely causes issues

❌ **Multiple Revision Rounds Beyond Package** (if you request extensive changes)
- Mitigation: Covered in Premium/Enterprise (more revision rounds included)

❌ **Specialized Equipment Needs** (thermal imaging, underwater drone, etc.)
- Mitigation: Flagged during discovery, planned accordingly

**Honest truth:** In 95% of projects, we deliver on schedule or early.

## Best Practices for Smooth Production

**To ensure your project stays on schedule:**

1. **Confirm access early**
   - Site access passes prepared
   - Key contacts briefed
   - Safety requirements communicated

2. **Provide reference materials**
   - Existing photos of infrastructure
   - Brand guidelines for graphics
   - Stakeholder interview list (if applicable)

3. **Make revision decisions quickly**
   - Review drafts within 2-3 days
   - Provide clear feedback (not vague)
   - Limit revision rounds to avoid scope creep

4. **Be available for production day**
   - Key contact on-site during filming
   - Access to sensitive areas arranged
   - Crew liaisons if needed

5. **Plan your deployment**
   - Think about how you'll use footage (LinkedIn? Investors? Employees?)
   - Prepare team for content rollout
   - Coordinate messaging with PR/communications
