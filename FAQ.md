# Frequently Asked Questions

## About GreenLand's Service

### What exactly do you do?

We create cinematic, professional documentation for maritime green infrastructure projects. We combine drone cinematography, industrial filming, and post-production editing to turn technical infrastructure into investor-ready, regulator-friendly visual proof.

**In short:** We make your green infrastructure *visible* and *believable* to stakeholders who control funding, regulation, and operations.

---

### Who is your typical client?

**Primary:** Shipyards, ports, and shipping companies in Nordic region receiving Enova subsidies  
**Secondary:** Any maritime organization with green infrastructure (shore power, hydrogen, batteries, electrification)  
**Tertiary:** Green technology developers needing investor communications

**Company size:** Typically 50-2,000 employees, serious about environmental transition

---

### What infrastructure do you document?

✅ **Shore power systems** (vessel charging during dock)  
✅ **Battery infrastructure** (large-scale storage, vessel retrofit)  
✅ **Hydrogen systems** (production, storage, refueling)  
✅ **Port electrification** (grid upgrade, charging infrastructure)  
✅ **Vessel retrofit projects** (engine conversion, system integration)  
✅ **LNG conversion systems** (if part of green transition)  
✅ **Green industrial facilities** (any maritime-related green infrastructure)

**We specialize in:** Projects where the infrastructure is capital-intensive, technically complex, and requires investor/regulatory buy-in.
